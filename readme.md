My Splen Macropad is small keyboard that can be use to typing sentences or everyday uses. My Splen macropad has two columns, three rows, OLED display, and seven buttons. I use an app called Kicad to sketch and design my macropad. In kicad, I use the Schematic editor to create a layout of where I wanted my switches and diodes to go, then I went to pcb editor to arrange the switches and diodes to my liking. I went to Fusion to create 3D version of Macropad in its case.
